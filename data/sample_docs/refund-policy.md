# Acme Cloud refund policy (sample document)

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are issued as account credit only. Annual plans are refunded pro rata for the unused months if the cancellation happens in the first 90 days.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days. The money returns to the original payment method. Purchases made with a gift card are refunded as account credit.

Enterprise contracts follow the terms in the signed agreement and are not covered by this policy. Contact your account manager for any enterprise billing question.
